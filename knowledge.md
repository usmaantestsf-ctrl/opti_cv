# CV Tailoring Knowledge & Strict Length Optimization Guidelines

Add your custom tuning, model prompts, and domain rules here.
This file is dynamically appended to the AI system prompt on every tailoring request.

---

## 1. Universal Word Count & Length Parity Rule (Crucial)
- **Visual Footprint Preservation**: The tailored CV must match the exact layout, page height, and line count of the original document. No paragraph should spill over to a new line or leave empty space.
- **Summary / Profile / Objective Paragraphs**:
  - The new text MUST match the original word count within **±10%**.
  - Formula: If original paragraph has $N$ words, the tailored version must be between $N - 10\%$ and $N + 10\%$ words (or within $\pm 5$ words for short summaries).
  - Do NOT condense a multi-sentence summary into a single short sentence. Maintain the original sentence structure, swapping out generic phrasing for targeted JD keywords.
- **Achievement / Experience Bullets**:
  - The rewritten bullet must stay within **±10% character length** of the original.
  - Do not summarize detailed bullets into terse fragments.
  - Replace weak filler words with strong action verbs and relevant domain methodologies/tools from the JD to maintain identical line length.

## 2. Truthfulness & Fact Integrity
- **Truthfulness First**: Use only skills, qualifications, tools, companies, degrees, and metrics already present in the master CV.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Keep all numbers, percentages, and metrics exact. Never invent or alter quantitative data.
- **Universal Action Verbs**: Start bullet points with high-impact verbs appropriate to the field:
  - *Leadership & Management*: Led, Directed, Spearheaded, Championed, Formulated, Coordinated.
  - *Engineering & Technical*: Engineered, Architected, Automated, Implemented, Deployed, Hardened.
  - *Analysis & Strategy*: Analyzed, Evaluated, Optimized, Forecasted, Identified, Streamlined.
  - *Execution & Delivery*: Delivered, Accelerated, Negotiated, Produced, Executed, Standardized.
- **Avoid Clichés**: Remove passive or filler phrases like *"Responsible for"*, *"Worked on"*, *"Helped with"*, *"Successfully demonstrated"*, *"Hard-working team player"*.

## 3. Section-Specific Rules
- **Headline (H flag)**: Tailor to the target job title plus 1-2 relevant specialties present in the CV. Keep within 70 characters.
- **Skills Lines**:
  - Preserve the exact label prefix (e.g., `Languages:`, `Core Competencies:`, `Tools:`).
  - Reorder the items so the most critical JD requirements appear first.
  - Do not drop or invent any skill.
- **Experience Bullets**:
  - Emphasize the top 3-4 bullets per role that directly address the job requirements.
  - Place JD keywords in the first 5 words of each rewritten bullet.
